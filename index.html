📘 The course

A real course, not a pile of flashcards.

Five levels of fifteen units, following the progression used by international children's English courses — from very first words to A2-style reading and writing. Every unit is a little lesson with the same friendly shape, so your child always knows where they are and what comes next.

  • 1 · Words20 illustrated words, spoken and practised
  • 2 · Phonics9 sounds and letters, up to the magic e
  • 3 · Grammar20 questions that teach the unit's pattern
  • 4 · StoryA 20-page illustrated story to read and hear
  • 5 · Think10 sums and 10 puzzles on the unit's pictures
  • 6 · Game TimeEight games played on that unit's words
  • 7 · QuizTen questions, three stars, maybe a medal
＋ Talk — in the fifteen conversation units ＋ Read — Levels 4 and 5 ＋ Write — Levels 4 and 5
Level 1First words

Hello!, toys, my body, family, food I like — and the very first conversations.

Level 2My world

Feelings, days and weather, my town, the farm, the zoo, going shopping.

Level 3Bigger sentences

Past and future, comparisons, the time, seasons — stories with a twist at the end.

Level 4 · Our WorldGeography begins

Our country, mountains and rivers, weather around the world, finding the way.

Level 5 · Bigger IdeasReady for anything

The seven continents, cities, space, looking after nature — and saying goodbye in English.

✨ What's inside

Everything a first English course should have — and a few things none of them do.

🎤

Say it out loud

Tap the microphone on any word card or story page, repeat what you heard, and a circle fills in with the score. Listening happens on the device alone — nothing is recorded, nothing is sent anywhere.

🌍

Your language first, English second

Every hint, story page and question follows the language you choose — and the app speaks yours first, then English. Or the other way round. Or English only, when your child is ready.

💬

Fifteen real conversations

In the Talk units a scene plays out in two voices — then the roles swap, and your child speaks their own lines out loud while the app plays the other part.

📖

Read — a story alone

From Level 4, your child reads five paragraphs by themselves, then answers five questions about what happened. The first quiet, proud steps of real reading.

✏️

Write — real sentences, typed

The Write step asks for whole sentences on the keyboard and checks them word by word, showing exactly which words landed and which need another look.

🧠

Think — maths and logic, in English

After every story: ten sums counted out on the unit's own pictures, and ten puzzles — what comes next, which one doesn't belong. From counting to ten up to sums to a hundred.

🎮

Ten kinds of game

Memory Match, Listen and Tap, Word Builder, Sentence Builder, Balloon Pop, Odd One Out, Missing Letter, True or False, Word Sort and the Quiz. Score 90% and a gold medal arrives with confetti.

🗺️

Geography that knows your home

Levels 4 and 5 teach countries, rivers, continents and the way home — and the questions follow your language. Choose French, and your child is asked about France and Paris.

🧒

Your own child, in the app

Type a name and choose a photo, and both appear everywhere — the home screen, the games, the stories and the medal ceremony. The photo never leaves the device.

📊

A learning report for parents

One tap on the green ring shows how much has been learned, the practice streak, and a bar for every skill — so you can see what is going well and what needs another go.

🌈

Twelve Bonus worlds

Free-play topics beside the course — ABC and phonics, numbers, colours, animals, food, greetings, months and days, family and body, school things, actions, weather and clothes.

⭐

2,199 stars to collect

Every step is worth up to three stars, every great score a gold medal. Progress is saved on the device — doing a step again is always allowed, and the best result stays.

Questions parents ask

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is a free download, Level 1 Unit 1 is free for ever, and one single payment opens everything else. It never renews, and it can be restored free on any device signed into the same Apple Account.

How much does the full course cost?

€39.99 in the euro area, £34.99 in the UK, US$34.99 in the United States, 199,99 lei in Romania and US$39.99 in Moldova. It is paid once, for all seventy-five units, the twelve Bonus worlds, every story, game and conversation, on both iPhone and iPad. The App Store shows the price in your own currency, and a new device costs nothing: Restore purchases brings it back free. There is nothing else to buy, ever.

How do I open the full course?

On the home screen tap Open the whole course, then I am a grown-up, and answer the multiplication question. Only then do the price, the Unlock everything button and Restore purchases appear. The question is there because this is a children's app, and a child should never reach a payment alone.

What ages is it for?

Six to ten. Level 1 assumes nothing at all — a six-year-old who has never met English can start there. Levels 4 and 5 are real A2-style work, with reading and writing, so the course keeps up as your child grows.

Does my child need to read already?

No. Everything is spoken out loud, every word has a picture, and the phonics steps teach reading from single letters up to the spelling patterns of real sentences.

Does it need the internet?

Only for the purchase itself, which Apple handles. The lessons, stories, games and the pronunciation coach all work with no connection at all — on a plane, at grandma's village, anywhere.

Which languages can it explain things in?

Romanian, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Ukrainian and Mandarin — completely: every hint, story, question and parent screen, plus the second voice. English itself is taught with a British voice.

What do you collect about my child?

Nothing, about anyone, of any age. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party code. The name and photo you add stay in the app's own folder on your device, and what the microphone hears is understood on the device and never stored or sent.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or iPadOS 17, or later. The course is the same on both. For a child, the bigger screen of an iPad is the more comfortable one.

Is there an Android version?

No. The app is available for iPhone and iPad only.

Where do I download it?

On the App Store. The button at the top of this page opens it directly, and the app is listed there as My First English for Kids. The download is free. Questions are welcome at adrian.lupusor@gmail.com, and you will get a reply from a person.
